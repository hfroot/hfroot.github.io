Welcome to my flat - a guide

You are under no obligation to read this btw

Entering the building

There is a buzzer at the building entrance

There are no lifts, I can give you a hand with your bags if you'd like!

I will give you keys to the flat, a smaller silver one is for the building door and the bigger one is for my flat. The door locks automatically as you leave but please deadbolt it as well if you leave.

I avoid using the overhead lights as much as possible, they are gross. Give me a heads up if I am in the room and you're about to turn the lights on plz.

The sounds between the flats can travel a bit - Sometimes when my neighbour leaves and enters it sounds like it's coming from my front door.

Bedroom

You will sleep here There isn't much in the way of storage space

It is pretty noisy at night, so you have the fun choice between hotter but quieter (windows closed) or louder and cooler (you've guessed it). The secret third option is opening the windows but closing the blinds.

I have melatonin gummies (they are a culinary treat) if you struggle to sleep, I'll leave them in the bathroom, you can help yourself to them.

The blinds are closed by pulling the flat chord towards your body and then lowering the shutters down.

Kitchen

The door to the window is hard to close, you really have to push the door in and jam the handle down, and it generally goes 45 degrees not the full 90. You can probably just leave it open tbh.

Help yourself to any food you can find

For coffee I have a vietnamese drip and an aeropress. Coffee is in a plastic container in the top shelf of the fridge, I leave the scoop by the toaster. I will try to stock up on tonic water as well if I can convince you to join the dark side. Ice in the freezer, from bag or tray.

Bathroom

You can lock the door at your own risk - the risk being that the door handle gets kinda stuck and you have to rattle it around a bit to get it unlocked. I generally just assume if the door is closed there is someone inside

The fan is controlled by one of the overhead light switches, so please leave the lights on for a while after you have showered

The water in the sinks doesn't get very warm no matter how long you wait. You have better luck on the right hand sink

There is a lamp with a switch on the wire on the opposite side to the lamp than the entrance. I recommend using this light at night and not the overhead if you don't want to disrupt your cercadian rhythm with the glare of the radioactive suns they have installed in the ceiling
